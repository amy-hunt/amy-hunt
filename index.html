About Amy Hunt - Best-Selling Billionaire Romance Author

Welcome! I'm Amy Hunt, a best-selling contemporary romance author specializing in steamy billionaire romance novels based in sunny Florida. Living in a cozy cottage near the beach with my husband, I draw endless inspiration from romantic books, movies, and the beautiful coastal setting that surrounds me.

My passion is crafting compelling love stories that feature irresistible billionaire heroes, grumpy alpha males, enemies-to-lovers chemistry, small-town settings, and fierce sassy heroines who give as good as they get. Whether you love sports romance, fake dating, secret baby storylines, brother's best friend tropes, or forbidden off-limits romances, my books deliver all the feels with plenty of heat.

Every single one of my contemporary romance novels promises a guaranteed happily ever after (HEA) because I believe love should always win. Perfect for fans of steamy billionaire romance, alpha male protectors, and swoon-worthy love stories that will keep you reading late into the night.
